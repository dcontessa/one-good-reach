## Full description

One Good Reach helps you move from reflection to real human connection.

Start with a quick Soul, Mind, and Body check-in. The app then suggests one safe, achievable action that takes about five minutes. You can prepare an editable message or conversation for someone who matters, complete the reach in your own time, and reflect privately afterward.

ONE SMALL STEP TOWARD SOMEONE WHO MATTERS

Choose from gentle forms of reconnection such as:

• Expressing gratitude
• Checking in on someone
• Making a simple invitation
• Asking for a little support
• Preparing a thoughtful repair

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your check-ins, personal labels, prepared messages, and reflections stay on your device. One Good Reach does not access your contacts, send messages automatically, or create a public profile.

CALM, NOT COMPETITIVE

There are no public feeds, leaderboards, or streak pressure. The app is built around one quiet daily ritual and one achievable real-world action.

PREMIUM WHEN YOU WANT TO GO DEEPER

The useful daily core remains available without payment. Premium adds deeper pattern insights, additional guided reconnection journeys, relationship-specific plans, difficult-conversation preparation, and multilingual content. Purchases and restoration are managed securely through Google Play and RevenueCat.

SAFETY BOUNDARY

One Good Reach is a wellbeing and relationship-support tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis care. If crisis language is detected, the normal suggestion flow stops and the app directs the user toward immediate human support and crisis resources.

Technology should strengthen real relationships, not replace them. One Good Reach helps you take the first small step.
