ONE GOOD REACH

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

One Good Reach is designed to help people reflect privately and take small actions that strengthen real relationships. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

The app may store Soul, Mind, and Body ratings, optional private notes, person labels you create, prepared messages, action suggestions, completion reflections, history, and preferences locally on your device. This personal content is not uploaded to a One Good Reach server.

Purchases

One Good Reach uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to offer and manage optional Premium subscriptions. Google and RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, product information, device or app identifiers, and transaction metadata needed to complete purchases and restore access. One Good Reach does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Contacts and messages

One Good Reach does not access your address book. Person labels are entered manually and stored locally. The app does not send messages automatically. You remain in control of whether, when, and how you contact another person.

Advertising

The app does not use an advertising SDK and does not sell personal information for advertising.

Safety

One Good Reach is a wellbeing and relationship-support tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis care. A local safety check may identify crisis-related language and redirect the user toward immediate human support or crisis resources.

Data deletion

You can delete locally stored app data from the app's Settings screen. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data, subject to your device and backup settings. Purchase records are managed by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their own retention obligations.

Children's privacy

One Good Reach is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a One Good Reach server.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app evolves. For privacy questions, contact dcontessa100@gmail.com.
